Questions Usually Asked By Users

Find answers to common questions about CreditMesh.

What is CreditMesh?

CreditMesh is a financial inclusion platform that helps users build a financial identity using their authorized financial behaviour such as income patterns, payment history, savings habits, and cash flow information.

How does CreditMesh calculate my Financial Reliability Score?

CreditMesh analyses income consistency, payment reliability, savings behaviour, transaction activity, and cash flow stability to generate an explainable financial profile.

Does CreditMesh provide loans directly?

No. CreditMesh does not directly provide loans. It connects users with potentially suitable financial options from financial institutions.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. CreditMesh follows a privacy-first approach where users control their data permissions and decide what information can be accessed.

How can I improve my financial profile?

You can improve your profile by maintaining regular payments, managing expenses, building savings habits, and maintaining consistent financial records.
