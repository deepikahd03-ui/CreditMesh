Frequently Asked Questions

What is CreditMesh?
CreditMesh is an informational profile provider that helps you explore financial products from verified institutions. We do not approve, reject, or guarantee any financial product.
How does eligibility check work?
We compare your financial profile (income, business vintage, etc.) against the product's basic requirements. Meeting these criteria does not guarantee approval — the final decision is always made by the financial institution.
Is my data shared with institutions?
Your data is shared only with your explicit consent when you apply for a product. You can manage your data sharing permissions at any time from the Manage Permissions page.
What happens after I apply?
After submission, the financial institution will review your application, verify your documents, and communicate the decision directly to you. CreditMesh does not participate in the approval process.
Are the rates shown current?
The rates displayed are demo data for prototype purposes. Actual rates, eligibility, and terms are determined solely by the respective financial institution. Always verify current rates with the institution.

Need more help?
